Finding us

Downtown Wheaton, on Front Street.

Front Street runs along the south side of the railway through the middle of downtown Wheaton. We are on the block west of Main Street, one block from City Hall.

Coming from Chicago, the Union Pacific West Metra line stops in downtown Wheaton and the station is a short walk away.

Good to know

Before you come

First time eating Burmese?

Say so. Order a tea leaf salad, one curry with rice, and a noodle for the table — that is a proper Burmese meal and it covers everything the kitchen does well.

Vegetarian or gluten-free?

Plenty of the menu is both, and you can filter it. Do tell us when you order though — several Burmese dishes are built on shrimp paste or fish sauce that isn't obvious from the name.

Bringing a group?

Burmese food is built for it — everything is put down in the middle and shared. Give us a call first so we know you're coming.
